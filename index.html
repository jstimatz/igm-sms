SMS Notifications Opt-In

This form lets authorized staff of Integrity Glass and Metal, Inc opt in to receive SMS text messages from the company's internal operations assistant. Integrity Glass and Metal, Inc is the sole sender and operator of this messaging program. No other company is involved in or sends messages through this program.

How consent works

Authorized staff enter their own mobile number and check the consent box(es) above. Service-message consent and marketing consent are collected as two separate checkboxes — marketing consent is optional and is never bundled with, or required for, service consent. Integrity Glass and Metal, Inc messages only the individuals who have explicitly opted in. Phone numbers are never purchased, sold, or shared with third parties.

Opt-out & help

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from all messages. Reply START to opt back in. Reply HELP for assistance.
