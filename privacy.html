SMS Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Integrity Glass and Metal, Inc ("we," "us," "our," or "the Company") collects and uses mobile phone information for its SMS text messaging program. Integrity Glass and Metal, Inc is the sole operator of this program.

1. Information we collect

We collect the name and mobile phone number you provide on the Integrity Glass and Metal, Inc SMS opt-in form, along with the consent choices you make (service-message consent and, optionally, marketing consent).

2. How we use it

Mobile phone numbers and opt-in data collected here are used solely by Integrity Glass and Metal, Inc to deliver the SMS messages you consent to.

3. No sharing of mobile information

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile information or mobile opt-in data with any third party or affiliate for any purpose, including their marketing purposes. No mobile opt-in data is shared with any third party. Mobile information is used only by Integrity Glass and Metal, Inc to operate this messaging program.

4. Separate marketing consent

Marketing or promotional SMS consent is collected separately from service-message consent, as a distinct and optional checkbox on the opt-in form. Marketing consent is never combined with, bundled with, or required as a condition of, service-message consent. Opting in to service messages does not opt you in to marketing messages, and you may provide service consent without providing marketing consent.

5. Opt-out

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from all messages. Reply HELP for assistance. Message frequency varies and message & data rates may apply.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact Integrity Glass and Metal, Inc.
